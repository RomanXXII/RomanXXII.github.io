About

Building at the intersection of AI and cybersecurity.

I recently graduated summa cum laude with a B.S. in Computer Science from the University of Oklahoma. This fall, I'll be attending Auburn University to pursue an M.S. in Cybersecurity Engineering. War Eagle!

My work explores how intelligent systems can be evaluated, secured, and deployed safely within complex technical environments, with interests spanning machine learning security, autonomous systems, and resilient cyber infrastructure. I am especially interested in the challenges that emerge as these systems become increasingly embedded in everyday life.

Graduate focus
Preparing for security engineering work with a focus on AI security, reverse engineering, and the reliability of increasingly autonomous systems.
Questions I chase
How do AI systems fail, how can we evaluate their behavior, and what makes people able to actually trust them in real-world environments?
What I build toward
Secure and understandable systems that help engineers inspect, evaluate, and safely integrate AI into the technologies people use every day.
